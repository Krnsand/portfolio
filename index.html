About me

I’m a creative and social frontend developer who loves building things that are both functional and beautiful. With experience in web development and full-stack technologies, I love turning ideas into intuitive interfaces that feel good to use. My background in administration has also given me strong communication skills and an understanding of how people and technology work together. When I’m not coding, you’ll usually find me creating something with my hands, listening to music, caring for plants, experimenting in the kitchen, or spending time with animals. These passions fuel my creativity and help me bring a thoughtful, balanced perspective to my work.

Projects

Klockspelet

Read more

Klockspelet is an interactive light and sound installation in Gothenburg, Sweden, that reflects the rhythm of the day and brings energy to its surroundings during the darkest hours of the year. As the city’s great clock, it invites you to make the most of your time instead of simply letting it tick away. A symbolic heart that beats to spread warmth. Klockspelet is made as a collaboration by Jens Thoms Ivarsson, Stadsmiljöförvaltningen Göteborg and Jimmy Herdberg and the Kokokaka Team.
